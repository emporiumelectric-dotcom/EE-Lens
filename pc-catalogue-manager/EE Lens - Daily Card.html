Adding a product on the PC

1
Import from product URL
Paste one or more links, one per line. Enter makes a new line. Click Fetch details.
2
Check what came back
Fix anything wrong. Pick the right SKU if the page sells several colours or sizes. Untick images you do not want.
3
Approve and save
Nothing is stored until you press this. The form then clears, ready for the next one.

No link? Use New product and type it. Brand and name are the only fields you must fill. Pick a category and the right fields for it appear — wattage, jars, cores, capacity.

Getting it to the phone

4
PC → Phone sync → Share this catalogue with the phone
Puts your catalogue where the phone can collect it.
5
Phone → Backup → Get from PC
Choose Add new only. You see what is coming before anything is saved.

Shop photos on the phone

6
Products → tap the product → Edit
Add 6–10 shop photos of the real product on your shelf, from a few angles. These are what the camera matches against — customers never see them.
7
Save
Recognition updates itself. Pull down on the Products list to force it.

Sending your work back

8
Phone → Backup → Send to PC
Then on the PC: Phone sync → Load what the phone shared.
9
Choose Replace existing
Replace existing — the phone now has more than the PC does, so the PC takes the phone's version.

The one rule to remember

Phone always picks “Add new only”. PC always picks “Replace existing”.

Shop photos live only on the phone until you send them across. If the phone ever picks “Replace existing”, the PC’s copy — which has no shop photos — writes over them and they are gone.

Backups

Sync copies between two devices. It is not a backup. A backup is a file you keep.

Two things that lose data

Uninstalling the app wipes everything on the phone. Android works that way and nothing can prevent it.

Clearing browser data wipes the PC catalogue. It lives inside the browser, and it does not follow you to another browser or computer.

An exported .eelens file survives both. Keep a few.

When something looks wrong

What you seeWhat it means
No Phone sync button on the PCYou opened the plain launcher. Close the black window and use the “(with phone sync)” one.
Phone says it cannot reach the PCBlack window closed, or the two are on different Wi-Fi.
Phone says wrong pairing codeThe code changes every time the black window is reopened. Read it fresh from Phone sync.
Sent from one side, nothing appeared on the otherSending only offers it. The other side still has to press its collect button.
Changes you made are not showingPress Ctrl+Shift+R in the browser, or pull down on the phone's Products list.
